# Mission: pipeline-actionable-crm
**Agent:** ProductSurface
**Type:** build
**Status:** in_progress

## Goal

Make `/pipeline` an actionable sales workspace: brighter company names, 15 customer opportunities unless Pro, no duplicate/confusing setup panels, no empty CRM stages, and an obvious CRM next step.

## Conversion hypothesis

Users bounce because the page looks like a classified listing (tiny type, empty New Signal/Discovered columns, “Draft Ready” before CRM is on). A ranked 15-buyer list plus one CRM activation CTA will increase first save.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Company names are high-contrast (near-white) and larger in the list and workspace
- [ ] Anonymous and Free see 15 opportunities; Pro/paid sees the full market
- [ ] Remove “You’re activated — finish setup” (duplicate of Search pipeline next steps)
- [ ] Hide empty New Signal / Discovered columns; do not present SIGNAL feed as CRM stages before CRM is activated
- [ ] CRM activation is the primary next step for signed-in users with 0 saves
- [ ] Right panel reads as “Work this buyer”, not a tiny classified clipping
