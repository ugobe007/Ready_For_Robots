# Formalize the Robot Employment Model; first RobCo job cards

**Date:** 2026-08-22  
**Type:** build  
**Agents:** ProductThesis, ProductSurface

## Goal

ReadyForRobots is recruitment + placement infrastructure for robotic labor. The unit of value is the Robot Job, not a lead. Formalize that as the operating model, render Jobs as employment Job Cards, and pack the first evidence-backed machine-tending cards for RobCo.

## Acceptance

1. `docs/robot_employment_model.md` is the canonical loop and language (never lead/prospect/closed-won).
2. Expanded Jobs cards show Employer, Workplace, Work, Qualification, Open questions, Next step — no invented labor dollars.
3. RobCo pack uses named machine-tending work from the corpus/ledger. Do not fabricate employers to hit 10.
4. Vitest covers the Job Card mapper and pack honesty (no fake FTE / payback).

## Out of scope

Marketplace posting, MATCH scoring changes, SIGNAL/Cal expansion, invented economics, employer-side UI.
