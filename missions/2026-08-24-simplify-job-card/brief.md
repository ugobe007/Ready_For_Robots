# Simplify the Robot Job Card

**Date:** 2026-08-24  
**Type:** build  
**Agents:** ProductSurface

## Goal

The expanded Job Card is a posting, not a research dump. Show the work, the required task model, three model links, and three questions. Hide qualify filters and pricing until a later step.

## Acceptance

1. Expanded card: employer, workplace, work, qualification, task-model slot, **3 model links**, why listed, **3 open questions**, site-assessment next step.
2. No “How we qualify a candidate”, no “Where to find price”, no survey/talent/price URLs, no long notes.
3. Model links are clickable VLA / Isaac / OEM-weight destinations. Presence stays unknown.

## Out of scope

SIGNAL. Invented prices. A model marketplace.
