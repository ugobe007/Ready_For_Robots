## Discovery-call script (Stage 1 → qualify against BETA-READY)

Open: *"Before I show you anything — tell me about {{Task}} today: volume, who does it, what's hard about staffing it."*
Qualify (map to the rubric):
- **Task match:** "Is {{Task}} the same every time, or does it vary?"
- **Environment:** "Walk me through the station — space, power, layout."
- **Champion & speed:** "If a pilot went well, who says yes, and how fast?"
- **Commitment:** "Could your team give ~2 hrs/week of feedback and let us capture task data? Would you be a reference if it works?"
- **Pain & volume:** "What's turnover on that station? What does an open req cost you?"
Close: *"Based on that, {{Task}} looks like a strong fit. Next step is a 20-minute demo of the robot doing exactly that — then, if it lands, a no-cost 2–6 week validation at one site. Want me to set that up?"*
