# Place is the money moment — not a stale CRM dump

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Type:** build  
**Agents:** ProductSurface

## Goal

Step 03 currently lands like a pipeline dump: blocked jobs, “so what?”. Rebuild it as the **punchline** of FIND → QUALIFY → PLACE. Reevo/Monaco feel inevitable because every screen has full deal context and one next action. We copy that *feeling* for **robot jobs**, not their sales-CRM category. Cal stays frozen.

## Acceptance

1. Process bar 01 / 02 / 03 continues onto the desk. 03 is **Place the robot**.
2. Punchline names the robot, the employers, and the money move (quote rental → apply). No invented dollars.
3. Selected job is a deal: agent brief (what we know + your move), then Pack → Quote → Apply as the only current step.
4. Score strip: quoted n/5 and applied n/5 from user-entered rentals only.
5. No SIGNAL buyers. No OEM roster. No Cal.

## Out of scope

Reevo-style TAM, sequences, forecasting. Cal buyer-sales. Invented FTE or pack prices.
