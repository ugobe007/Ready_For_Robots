## Publish copy

TODAY'S JOB FOR A ROBOT

Unload packed cases from the conveyor and stack them onto pallets

Packaging plant · Kinston, North Carolina

At Novolex (Pactiv Evergreen) in Kinston, North Carolina, the palletizer role describes a concrete loop: **retrieve packed cases from conveyors, stack them onto pallets to carton specifications, wrap the load, and pull the skid.**

What we found isn't “a factory that could use a palletizer.” It's a named plant where that case → pallet handoff is written into the job — where the case comes from (the conveyor), where it goes (the pallet pattern), and what finishes the cycle (wrap and pull).

Robot work: pick those packed cases from the conveyor path and place them onto the outbound pallet.

Still unknown: case weight and dimensions, how often the pallet pattern changes, gripper needs, line speed, and how much of this cell is already automated.

Could your robot do this?
