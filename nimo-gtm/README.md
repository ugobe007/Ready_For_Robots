## The one-paragraph thesis

NIMO is tactile-first ("feels"), kitchen-specialized, and post-PoC — everyone else is vision-first and general-purpose. Cal targets controlled, high-repetition, labor-pained kitchens (ghost kitchens + QSR innovation labs first), offers a **no-cost validation pilot** on one real workflow, and converts validated betas into design-partner LOIs + published case studies.

## Workflow

1. Load `02_target_accounts.md` into the CRM; enrich + SIGNAL-score each row (verify why-now).
2. Route Fit=H accounts by persona to the matching sequence in `03`.
3. Qualify replies in Discovery against the **BETA-READY** rubric (`01`, ≥ 7/10 to pursue).
4. Demo → sign a no-cost beta MOU with agreed success metrics → 2–6 wk validation → convert.

**90-day north star:** 3 validated beta sites with signed reference rights.

> Why-now signals in `02` are directional hypotheses — verify before sending.
