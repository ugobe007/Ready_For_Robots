TODAY'S JOB FOR A ROBOT

Deliver finished kits to the production line

Aerospace manufacturing · Foley, Alabama

At Collins Aerospace in Foley, Alabama, the work includes **kitting and delivering finished kits to the production line** — a named handoff from kit build to where the aircraft parts are assembled.

That caught our attention because it isn't a vague “material handling” job. It's a repeatable move of a completed physical object between known points: kitting area → line.

Robot work: move completed kits from the kitting area to point-of-use on the line along that path.

Still unknown: kit weight and size, clean/controlled-area rules, line-side staging layout, traffic with other plant vehicles, and whether any automation already covers kit delivery.
