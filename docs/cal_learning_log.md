## How to add a row

1. Paste the broken line or behavior under **Original**.
2. Paste what worked under **Correction**.
3. Write one sentence **Why**.
4. Write a **general** rule (not “fix PFG email”).
5. Fill **Outcome** when known (reply, requirements, meeting, or “template updated”).
6. If the rule is durable, promote into [cal_voice_and_persona.md](./cal_voice_and_persona.md) and [cal_persona_spec.md](./cal_persona_spec.md).
