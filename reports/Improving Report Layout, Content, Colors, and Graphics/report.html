Market Overview

Humanoid vendors continue to ship faster hardware and louder demos, while enterprise buyers still ask the same question: who is actually running pilots, with which customers, and on what timeline. This edition scores 103 humanoids on the HEIF framework (mobility, manipulation, cognition, safety, data pipeline, production) and cross-checks each against public deployment signals — catalog status, estimated commercial deployments, and press coverage where available.

Across the full index, 54.4% show PoC-or-better field evidence and 9.7% show commercial or fleet-scale signals. That gap between "capable on paper" and "proven in operation" is the central story: 0 robots score primarily from specs with little or no deployment corroboration.

Key Findings

Index Leader: Dexmate Vega (Dexmate) tops the composite index at 77 (HEIF 3.1/4), led by manipulation, production, cognition. Scores are inferred from published specs rather than HEIR primary research. Deployment tier: Commercial deployment.
Tight Race at the Top: Agibot A2 ties or nearly ties the leader on index score (77 vs 77). Differentiate on deployment tier (Commercial deployment vs Commercial deployment) and dimension strengths, not headline index alone.
No Single "Best" Humanoid: 6 different robots lead at least one HEIF dimension. Mobility is led by Boston Dynamics Atlas (Boston Dynamics); manipulation by Dexmate Vega (Dexmate). Buyers should short-list by task (locomotion vs dexterous work) rather than a single leaderboard rank.
Capability Ahead of Proof: 5 robots combine HEIF ≥2.5 with PoC-or-weaker deployment evidence — including Agibot G5, Tesla Optimus Gen 2, Tesla Optimus Gen 1, Figure 03 and 1 others. These are watch-list platforms for engineering progress, not proven operators yet.
Vendor with Broadest Deployment Footprint: Unitree Robotics leads on combined signals: 2 robots with commercial/fleet evidence, 80 catalog-reported deployments across 3 indexed models (100.0% PoC-or-better).
Deployment-Weighted Leader Differs: When HEIF is weighted by logged deployments, Agibot A2 (Agibot (Zhiyuan Robotics)) rises to the top — not Dexmate Vega. Use both rankings: index for engineering potential, deployment-weighted for operational proof.

Competitive Dynamics & Deployment Funnel

The market splits into locomotion-first platforms (strong gait, weaker manipulation evidence), manipulation-first systems (dexterity and payload drive value), and AI-first stacks where cognition scores run ahead of production readiness. The dimension-leader table in this report makes that split explicit.

Roughly 45.6% of the fleet still sits at demo-or-research tiers — the index is top-heavy with aspirational capability scores. Discount index rank when deployment tier is "research" or "demo only."

Deployment Funnel

Among robots with PoC-or-better evidence, about 0.18 also show commercial/fleet-class signals — the funnel from trial to scaled deployment remains narrow. 84 robots report zero catalog commercial deployments; index score alone does not imply installed base.
