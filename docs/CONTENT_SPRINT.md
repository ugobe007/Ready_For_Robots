## Quality gate (every post)

> What did we discover here that the reader probably didn't know before reading?

| Publish | Don't publish |
|---------|----------------|
| “Specialty-pharma outbound roles explicitly require returning totes/carts to operating areas.” | “Robots can move totes in warehouses.” |
| “Airport hiring overnight floor techs to run scrubbers across terminals.” | “Robots can clean airports.” |

If the answer to the gate is **nothing**, don't publish.

Also: **job is the headline** · no giant ReadyForRobots branding · no #RobotJob spam · no LWO/E1/ontology jargon. Audience gets: what we found · why it matters · what the robot could do · what we still don't know.
