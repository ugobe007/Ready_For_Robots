## 🎨 DESIGN SPECS FOR GRAPHICS

### Infographic Dimensions
- **LinkedIn:** 1200x1500px (portrait)
- **Instagram:** 1080x1080px (square) or 1080x1350px (portrait)
- **Twitter:** 1200x675px (landscape)

### Color Palette (from your site)
- Primary: Emerald (#10b981) / Cyan (#06b6d4)
- Accent: Purple (#8b5cf6)
- Background: Dark slate (#0f172a)
- Text: White / Gray

### Key Stats to Visualize
1. **62% Strong Intent** — Circular progress chart
2. **Industry Breakdown** — Horizontal bar chart (Logistics 42, Hospitality 33, etc.)
3. **Signal Types** — Donut chart (News 111, Labor 97, CAPEX 91, etc.)
4. **Perfect Storm** — Three overlapping circles (Labor, CAPEX, Hires)
5. **Market Opportunity** — $2.4B-$15.8B range visual

### Tools
- Canva templates (search "data infographic")
- Figma (for custom designs)
- DataWrapper (for interactive charts on Medium)
